# BearMiner pitch: 7-minute talk track

Ten slides, about 40 seconds each. Times are cumulative.

| # | Slide | Time | Say this |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BearMiner title | 0:00–0:20 | We're TempTeam. BearMiner digs Bern gold from life-science partners. It's built for the Bern Economic Development Agency and powered by Apertus. |
| 2 | Why Bern | 0:20–1:05 | Bern is Switzerland's federal city and wants to be a leading international medical location by 2030. It has Inselspital, a top-100 university, sitem-insel, ARTORG and Diabetes Center Berne, plus industry like Ypsomed and CSL Behring. The Bern Economic Development Agency brings companies here; BearMiner tells it which ones fit and who to introduce them to. |
| 3 | Problem | 1:05–1:40 | Finding an expert means knowing where to look first. Bern's research is spread over many sites, and everyone uses different words: "glucose sensors" vs CGM vs biosensing. |
| 4 | Two sides, how it works | 1:40–2:20 | Research side: rules crawl the three sources and keep quoted highlights. Company side: Apertus reads any pitch, even in Chinese, and builds a medical profile; rules score it. Both meet on evidence. |
| 5 | Company logic | 2:20–3:30 | Our core. Any pitch goes in: we read it, Apertus translates and builds a medical profile with a quote for every item, then rules match it to Bern and score six capped parts. Capability overlap weighs most because sector and country don't separate companies. 25+ earns one coin, 50+ two coins (P2), 70+ three coins (P1). Not life science scores 0. |
| 6 | Result | 3:30–4:20 | Left: a real record from our crawl, with quote and link. Right: a wearable-CGM company scores 85.5, three coins, P1. Every point is explained, and "why not higher" tells Bern what's missing: no Bern site yet. |
| 7 | AI + trust | 4:20–5:10 | Apertus does the language work; code decides the score and checks every claim. No quote, no item. No guessing. Location points only from verified sources. |
| 8 | Rigour | 5:10–5:50 | On our samples the score spreads from 87 down to 0, so it separates real fits from noise. A Chinese-language CGM maker still earns three coins at 72. Our data so far: 42 UniBE source folders, about 15,300 unique URLs crawled and 3,775 pages stored locally. Thirty labelled benchmark examples and tests for every gate back it up. |
| 9 | Sovereign | 5:50–6:25 | Runs on-premise or on Swiss cloud with Apertus, SQLite and Python; Apertus also translates, and the Google Translate fallback is switched off for sovereign deployments. No GPU for crawling or scoring. A new institution is one config line. |
| 10 | Pilot | 6:25–7:00 | Give us one quarter and one real company inbox: complete crawl, expert review, then live outreach. Thank you. |
