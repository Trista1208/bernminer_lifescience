# BearMiner pitch: 7-minute talk track

Ten slides, about 40 seconds each. Times are cumulative.

| # | Slide | Time | Say this |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BearMiner title | 0:00–0:20 | We're TempTeam. BearMiner digs Bern gold from life-science partners. It's built for the Bern Economic Development Agency and powered by Apertus. |
| 2 | Problem | 0:20–1:00 | Finding an expert means knowing where to look first. Bern's research is spread over UniBE, ARTORG and Diabetes Center Berne, and everyone uses different words: "glucose sensors" vs CGM vs biosensing. |
| 3 | Two sides | 1:00–1:40 | We solve both sides: what Bern can do (research highlights with evidence) and who Bern should call (companies scored for fit). They meet on evidence. |
| 4 | How it works | 1:40–2:20 | Public web in, source-backed answers out. Rules collect and select; Apertus understands language and explains matches. |
| 5 | Company logic | 2:20–3:30 | Our core. Any pitch goes in: we read it, Apertus translates and builds a medical profile with a quote for every item, then rules match it to Bern and score six capped parts. Capability overlap weighs most because sector and country don't separate companies. 25+ earns one coin, 50+ two coins (P2), 70+ three coins (P1). Not life science scores 0. |
| 6 | Result | 3:30–4:20 | Left: a real record from our crawl, with quote and link. Right: a wearable-CGM company scores 85.5, three coins, P1. Every point is explained, and "why not higher" tells Bern what's missing: no Bern site yet. |
| 7 | AI + trust | 4:20–5:10 | Apertus does the language work; code decides the score and checks every claim. No quote, no item. No guessing. Location points only from verified sources. |
| 8 | Rigour | 5:10–5:50 | On our samples the score spreads from 87 down to 0, so it separates real fits from noise. Thirty labelled benchmark examples, tests for every gate, and English, German and Japanese input. |
| 9 | Sovereign | 5:50–6:25 | Runs on-premise or on Swiss cloud with Apertus, SQLite and Python. No GPU for crawling or scoring. A new institution is one config line. |
| 10 | Pilot | 6:25–7:00 | Give us one quarter and one real company inbox: complete crawl, expert review, then live outreach. Thank you. |
